Full Video Submission Terms

DRAFT v2026-10-21 — NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED. These proposed terms must be approved, and real contact/privacy details supplied, before submissions open.

These terms describe how The Suno Country Music Spot (TSCMS) proposes to receive and use member-submitted music video files for Monday Music Video Night, the TSCMS website, and the Western Drive-In.

1. Eligibility and authority

You must be at least 18, authorized to submit the video, and able to grant the uses below. Obtain all necessary rights from other owners of music, sound recordings, performances, imagery, footage, and other included material.

2. Actual video files only

Submit the original video file, such as MP4 or MOV, through an approved Email or Messenger destination after the form is completed. Links to YouTube, Facebook, Suno or other streams do not substitute for files.

3. Permission to TSCMS

You retain ownership. You grant TSCMS non-exclusive, royalty-free permission to receive, securely store, technically process, review, and—if selected—display or stream your submitted video on the TSCMS site, during Monday Music Video Night, and at the TSCMS Western Drive-In. This permission allows reasonable format conversion needed for playback. It does not transfer ownership or authorize TSCMS to sell or sublicense your video to unrelated third parties.

4. Review and selection

Submission does not guarantee acceptance, scheduling, display, or indefinite storage. TSCMS may decline or stop showing videos for quality, rights, technical, suitability, or community-guideline reasons.

5. Copyright, AI-generated content and other people

Confirm any rules of the services used to make the song or video, including commercial use and redistribution. Obtain appropriate permission for third-party copyrighted material and people depicted in the video. Checking this box does not grant missing third-party rights.

6. Withdrawal and removal

You may ask TSCMS to stop future use of your video. After verifying the request, TSCMS intends to make reasonable efforts to remove hosted copies and stop future presentations. Past live streams or copies beyond TSCMS's control may not be reversible. A working contact address must be provided before launch.

7. Information, consent and privacy

The eventual system is intended to collect artist/member name, Facebook profile name, complete song title, optional notes, and a verifiable consent record (date/time and terms version). A privacy notice, access controls, retention/deletion policy, and consent-recording backend must be finalized before accepting submissions. This preview stores none of that data.

8. Changes

Material changes to uses covered by the permission should require a new affirmative agreement. The system should retain the version of terms accepted for each submission.

9. Questions and contact

Contact details for support and withdrawal are not yet confirmed and must be added before release.

This document is a draft for design review, not legal advice. Seek qualified legal review before launch.
