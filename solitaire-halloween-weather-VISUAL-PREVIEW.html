WELCOME TO SOLITAIRE

JOKER EDITION

HOW TO PLAY

Build each foundation from Ace to King by suit. Build tableau piles downward, alternating red and black. Only Kings may move into empty tableau spaces.

  • Choose Draw 1 or Draw 3.
  • Pause or Exit to preserve progress in the real game.

JOKER RULES

🃏 Two Jokers are shuffled into this game. They work on foundations only—not tableau piles. A Joker fills the next needed foundation card.

Find the real card later? Replace the Joker and reuse it from the waste pile.

MEET SWIPER

👻🎩

Swiper may appear during play. He can take an exposed top card and hide it beneath another tableau pile. The card is not destroyed, but the theft cannot be Undone.

An exposed Joker scares Swiper away.

⚡ A DEAL WITH THE SPIRITS ⚡

In Halloween mode with Swiper events enabled, the encounter setting rises from 25% to 35%. Swiper may relocate an exposed card beneath another tableau pile, and a successful theft cannot be Undone. An exposed Joker may scare him off.

These percentages and rules are descriptions of the existing planned engine—not a simulation on this visual preview page.
